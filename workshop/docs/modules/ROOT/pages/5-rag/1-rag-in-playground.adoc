= Try RAG in the playground

[role="_abstract"]
RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) is a technique where you feed information relevant to the current conversation into the prompt, so that the LLM has more context when it generates an answer. +
The playground you have been using is built on top of something called OGX (Open GenAI Stack) which you can also use for RAG, specifically splitting large documents into smaller chunks, ingesting them into a vector database, and retrieving them. +
You can use the LLM to answer specific questions, such as: +
`An infusion pump is showing error code 525. Who do I contact for a replacement and where do I send the faulty unit?`

Before you build your own RAG behavior, you can try out RAG with your model inside the Playground. +
You can upload documents and see the replies you get with your system prompt.

== Prerequisites

- The playground has been created according to xref:2-just-a-model/2-playground.adoc[2.2. Chat with your model in the playground] 

== Procedure

. Download the following file, it contains information about your hospital's standard operating procedures: link:https://github.com/rh-aiservices-bu/hospital-helpdesk/blob/main/oak_hospital_regulations.pdf[oak_hospital_regulations.pdf,window=_blank]

. Navigate to the Playground by going through *Gen AI studio* > *Playground*, and make sure you select your project.

. Click the *Knowledge* tab and turn on the *RAG* toggle.
+
image::5-rag/enable-rag.png[Enable RAG]

. Click *Upload* under *Add files* and select the oak_hospital_regulations.pdf you just downloaded.

. Change the chunk length to 200 and overlap to 100 to get smaller chunks, then click *Upload*.
+
image::5-rag/upload-settings.png[Upload Settings]

. The upload will likely pause at 50% for a couple of minutes while the file is processed. Once complete, it should look something like this.
+
image::5-rag/pdf.uploaded.png[PDF Uploaded]

== Verification

. Start by making sure that your system prompt is loaded.

. Before testing with RAG, first turn off RAG so you can see how the model behaves without it. Ask this question:
+
[.lines_space]
[.console-input]
[source,text]
----
An infusion pump is showing error code 525.
Who do I contact for a replacement and where do I send the faulty unit?
----

. Create a new chat by clicking *+ New Chat*. Turn on RAG, and then ask the same question.

. Did you see a difference? With RAG turned on, you should see an answer similar to this:
+
.Example RAG response
====
The replacement procedure for a ClearFlow infusion pump with error code 525 is as follows:

. Remove the device from service and tag it as faulty.
. File a Level 3 ticket in MedTrack under "Medical Devices > Infusion Pump General Failure."
. Clean the unit and send it to Biomedical Engineering via the designated utility room bin.

For an immediate replacement unit, contact the Central Supply department at extension 4450. They will dispatch a ready-for-use unit to your floor. Do not contact Biomedical Engineering for an immediate replacement; their role is repair, not immediate dispatch.

After filing the ticket and securing a replacement, the faulty ClearFlow pump should be cleaned according to standard sanitation procedures and sent to the Biomedical Engineering department. Place the tagged unit in the designated "Biomedical Outgoing" bin, typically located in the floor's main utility room next to the clean supply closet.
====

. You can ask more questions, such as: where the Biomedical Engineering department is located. When you are done testing RAG you can move on to the next section.

The Playground is not connected to MLflow, so you won't see traces from here. In the next section where you implement RAG in code, you will.
